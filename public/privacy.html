Privacy Policy

Design Warehouse · last updated 25 August 2026

Design Warehouse is a personal tool for cataloguing screenshots of designs. It runs entirely on your own device. There is no server, no account with us, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The people who wrote this app never receive your data, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your designs — the images you paste or import, and the notes and specifications you write about them — are stored on the device you are using: in your browser's storage.
  • Preferences such as sort order and theme are stored the same way.
  • Nothing is transmitted anywhere unless you turn on Google Drive sync, described below.

Google sign-in and Drive sync (optional)

The app works fully without signing in. If you choose to sign in with Google, it requests these permissions:

  • openid, email, profile — to know which account is signed in, so each person's catalogue stays separate on a shared device. Your email address and name are stored on your own device only.
  • drive.file — to keep your designs in a folder named Design Warehouse in your own Google Drive. This permission grants access only to files this app itself creates. It cannot read, modify, or see any other file in your Drive.

Your designs are then held in your Google account, under Google's terms, and are shared with nobody else unless you share that folder yourself. Google's handling of that data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Design Warehouse's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The data is never sold, never used for advertising, and never transferred to anyone.

Where credentials are kept

The access token exists only in memory and is gone when you close the tab; no long-lived credential is stored. Your designs themselves are not encrypted, so anyone who can use your computer account or browser profile can read them, in the same way they could read any other file you own.

Deleting your data

  • Delete individual designs from within the app. A deleted design stops being listed and is removed from Drive straight away, but its copy on this device is kept for thirty days so the deletion can be undone, and is erased for good after that. Delete entire catalogue, in the sync panel, is immediate and cannot be undone.
  • Turn on Delete this device's copy when I sign out to remove the local copy automatically when you sign out.
  • Delete the Design Warehouse folder in your Google Drive to remove the synced copy.
  • Revoke the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

This tool is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone.
